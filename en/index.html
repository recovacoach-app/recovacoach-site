About the App

RecovaCoach is an iOS app for logging your workouts and tracking the recovery of each muscle group. It calculates how recovered each muscle group is from the exercises, weights, and reps you log, and suggests what to train today.

Recovery Tracking

Suggests today's workout based on the recovery status of each muscle group.

Workout Logging

Log exercises, weights, reps, and sets, and automatically detect new personal bests.

Routines

Automatically detects the exercise combinations you use often, so you can start logging them with one tap next time.

Stats & History

Review your weekly volume trends and muscle balance.

Privacy Policy

The App handles the following information.

TypeDetailsStored in / Sent to

Usage analytics Screen views, feature usage, and how you heard about the App (only if you answer the in-app question) (in a form that does not personally identify you) PostHog (third-party analytics service)
